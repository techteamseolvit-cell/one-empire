Our story

Two different paths, one mission: helping Latino contractors build companies that grow.

Two paths, one mission

01

The meeting

Some encounters look like chance, but over time they turn out to be part of a much bigger purpose, and that's exactly what happened when Hugo Pérez and Mónica Tuccella met at a coaching certification. Both had arrived with the same hunger to grow, to become better leaders and to take their businesses to another level, so they quickly recognized each other. What began as a conversation between peers became a real alliance: when the certification ended they decided to be accountability buddies, committing to push each other, to hit every goal they set and not let either one settle for less than they were capable of.

02

Hugo and Potros Construction

Hugo already knew construction from the inside. He had built Potros Construction through hard work; he knew what it meant to be on the job site, lead his crews, deliver for a client and, above all, sit across from a homeowner and close a contract. His sales talent had taken him far, but like the vast majority of contractors, his growth depended too much on referrals and on the effort he could put in each day. Hugo knew his company had far more potential, and he also knew that to unlock it he needed someone who understood marketing and strategy as deeply as he understood construction.

03

Mónica and SEOLVIT

That person was Mónica Tuccella. Founder of SEOLVIT, a digital marketing agency recognized with the prestigious dotCOMM Gold award, Mónica has built her career helping companies turn their marketing strategies into real, sustainable and measurable growth.

Throughout her career, her strategies have helped generate more than 180 million dollars in results for her clients, leading her to work with multimillion-dollar companies and become a reference in marketing and business growth.

Her passion for business and her conviction that success is not a matter of luck but of vision, strategy and action inspired her to write El Camino Más Rápido al Éxito, a book in which she shares the principles and lessons that have shaped her path as an entrepreneur and that she now uses to drive the growth of others.

04

A result that changed everything

That's why, when Hugo decided to hire SEOLVIT to run Potros Construction's marketing, he did so with the confidence of someone who had already seen up close how Mónica thought and worked. What came next exceeded every expectation: in just three months, combining Mónica's strategy with Hugo's ability to close sales, Potros Construction went from billing $100,000 a month to over $600,000 a month. It wasn't luck or an isolated campaign, but proof of what happens when an experienced contractor eager to grow joins a marketing system designed to consistently attract the right clients.

That result changed everything, because it opened a conversation neither could ignore. If this had worked for Potros, why couldn't it work for the thousands of Latinos who, like Hugo, are excellent at what they do, work more hours than anyone and still feel their company isn't growing at the pace they deserve? Both knew that reality firsthand and knew our community has the talent, the work ethic and the drive, but often lacks the tools, the strategy and the guidance of someone who has already walked the path.

05

How ONE EMPIRE was born

That's how ONE EMPIRE was born, from the union of two forces rarely found in the same place: Hugo's construction experience and sales mastery, together with Mónica's marketing vision and business strategy. Together they created a space that is at once academy, consultancy and mentorship, where Latino contractors learn from people who don't speak from theory, but from the results they've already achieved.

06

Elite Builders
