Why isn't your construction company growing even though you work harder than anyone?

If you relate to more than one of these, your company is leaving thousands of dollars on the table every month.

  1. 01One month you have more work than you can handle, and the next you don't know where the next project will come from.
  2. 02Almost all your clients come from referrals, and when referrals dry up, the phone stops ringing.
  3. 03Leads come in, but most go with another contractor and you never know why.
  4. 04You send quotes over WhatsApp with just a number, and if the client doesn't reply, that's the end of it.
  5. 05You finish projects and invoice, but at the end of the month you don't know what you really earned.
  6. 06You compete on price because you feel that if you don't lower it, you don't close.
  7. 07You're the one who sells, quotes, supervises, collects and fixes everything, and if you stop, the company stops.
  8. 08You've invested in ads, websites or a marketing "expert" and saw no results.

You don't lack talent: your construction company lacks a system

Most Latino contractors we know are excellent on the job site. They know how to build, they deliver, and they work at a level of quality many big companies don't have. But at some point they realized that being the best at the work is not the same as having a company that grows, and that while they work twelve hours a day, less experienced competitors win the best contracts because they have a system to attract clients, close sales and charge what they're worth.

Every month without that system is not a "normal" month. It's a month in which your best leads called someone else, in which you accepted a project that left no profit, and in which your family saw less of you than they deserve.

You don't lack talent. You lack a system. And a system can be built.

Frequently asked questions about our mentorship for contractors

Does this work for my type of construction company?

We work with remodeling, roofing, painting, concrete, landscaping, flooring, masonry contractors and more. If you have a construction or home-services company, the principles are the same.

Do I need a minimum revenue to join?

On the call we review your current situation and tell you honestly whether we're the best option for you right now.

Is everything in Spanish?

Yes. All our content, mentorship and support are in Spanish.

I don't have time, I already work too much.

That's exactly why we exist. The goal is for you to stop working more hours and start working with a system that works for you.

I've invested in marketing before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?

Most contractors who come to us say the same. The problem is almost never marketing itself, but that there was no strategy, sales system or follow-up behind it. That's what we build with you.

Every company is different. On the call we understand where you are and what you need, and we present the option that makes sense for you.
